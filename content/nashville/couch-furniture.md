## Free: one piece per visit at a Convenience Center

Metro lists couches, chairs, sofas, dressers, mirrors, tables, toilets, vehicle seats and windows as bulk items it takes for disposal.

- **The free visit:** {{ fee_text('free_load') }}.
- **Extra pieces:** less than a cubic yard is {{ fee_text('volume_small') }}. Bigger loads are {{ fee_text('volume_large') }}.
- **Where:** any of the four centers: {{ centers() }}
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Closed on Metro holidays.
- **Bring:** a Tennessee driver's license with a Davidson County address. You unload your own load, and it must be household furniture, not from a business.

Before furniture goes to the landfill, Metro suggests seeing whether it can be reused, through a secondhand store or an online marketplace.

## Can't haul it? Schedule a curbside pickup

Metro's trash crews won't take furniture left at the curb, but Metro's bulky item page says to contact the **Davidson County Sheriff's Office** for curbside pickup of bulky items. The Sheriff's list includes old furniture, sofas and couches, lawn furniture and baby furniture.

- **Call 615-880-3897** to schedule. You'll be asked to list the items and give the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request.
- **Cost:** the Sheriff's pages we read don't mention a fee, so ask when you call.
- **Front curb, by 6:30 a.m.** Crews have up to seven business days from the date you're given (weekends and holidays don't count), so ask when to set things out.
- **No glass or mirrored items, and nothing in bags.** A mirror can go to a Convenience Center instead.
- **Apartment, condo or townhome?** The crews can't go onto complex property, so the Sheriff's Office asks that items go at the front curb by the property's main entrance.

Only put furniture out once you have a pickup date. Our [curbside pickup guide]({{ url('/nashville/bulk-trash/') }}) has the Sheriff's full list and rules.

## Donate it

**Habitat for Humanity ReStore**, 414 Harding Place, Nashville. Call 615-942-1291. ReStores resell donated furniture and appliances to fund Habitat's homebuilding, and Habitat says most offer pickup for large donations. Habitat also says upholstered and leather furniture has to be free of tears, stains and pet damage, and each ReStore's list is a little different. Call to see what they'll take and whether pickup is available in your area.
