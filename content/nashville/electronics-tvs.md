**Quick answer:** Recycling electronics is **free** at the East, Ezell Pike and Omohundro Convenience Centers, up to **100 pounds a day**. TVs can be up to 80 inches wide. Don't put electronics in your trash cart, because Metro bans them from household trash.
</div>

## What Metro takes

Phones and smartphones, computers and computer parts (cables, keyboards, mice, external drives), monitors, printers, copiers, scanners, fax machines, **TVs**, microwaves, stereos and radios, DVD and VCR players, tablets, e-readers, MP3 players and other handheld electronics, **rechargeable batteries**, and music or video cassettes and discs.

## The rules

- **Free**, with no fee at all.
- **Up to 100 lbs a day.** TVs can't be wider than 80 inches, and you can bring **one computer system a month**.
- **Household electronics only.** No business equipment, no truck or trailer loads, nothing in a work or commercial vehicle, and no out-of-county residents.
- **Bring** valid Davidson County ID.

**Where:** {{ centers('east', 'ezell', 'omohundro') }}

Hours are Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (Anderson Lane doesn't take electronics.)

## Before you drop it off

Wipe your phones, tablets and computers. Sign out of your accounts, do a factory reset, and take out any memory cards or discs you want to keep.
