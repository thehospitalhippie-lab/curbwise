<div class="answer" markdown="1">
**Quick answer:** Up to **4 tires a month** go to the East or Ezell Pike Convenience Center. Tires **without rims are free**. Tires still on rims cost **$5 each**.
</div>

## The details

- **Without rims:** {{ fee_text('tires') }}.
- **With rims:** {{ fee_text('tires_rims') }}.
- **Where:** {{ centers('east', 'ezell') }}
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Closed on Metro holidays.
- **Bring:** a Tennessee driver's license with a Davidson County address. These are for household tires only, not from a business.
- **Not accepted:** other car parts. Metro takes tires but no other automotive parts.

## Getting new tires?

Tire shops usually take your old tires when they put new ones on, often for a small disposal fee. If you're replacing them anyway, that's the easiest route.
