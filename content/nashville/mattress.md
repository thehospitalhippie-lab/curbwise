<div class="answer" markdown="1">
**Quick answer:** Take it to the East, Ezell Pike or Omohundro Convenience Center. It's **$12 per mattress or box spring**, up to 4 a month. Bring a Tennessee driver's license with a Davidson County address.
</div>

## Cheapest: drop it at a Convenience Center

- **Fee:** {{ fee_text('mattress') }}.
- **Where:** {{ centers('east', 'ezell', 'omohundro') }}
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Centers close on Metro holidays.
- **Bring:** a Tennessee driver's license showing a Davidson County address. You unload it yourself.
- **Paying:** cash works at East and Ezell Pike but not at Omohundro. Check or card works everywhere, and cards add a 2.8% fee (minimum $1.95).

Heads up: the free "3 bags plus 1 bulk item" visit **doesn't cover mattresses**. You'll pay the $12 even if it's the only thing you bring.

## Recycle it

Metro's list of local recycling providers includes **Spring Back**, a mattress recycler: 615-207-2736. Call to ask how drop-off or pickup works.

## Buying a new one?

Many mattress stores and online sellers will take your old mattress away when they deliver the new one, usually for a fee. Ask before you check out, because it's often the easiest option of all.

## Donating

Charities are picky about used mattresses. Most won't take one with stains or tears, so call before you load it up.
