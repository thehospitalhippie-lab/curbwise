## Drop it off at a Convenience Center

- **Fee:** {{ fee_text('mattress') }}.
- **Where:** {{ centers('east', 'ezell', 'omohundro') }}
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Centers close on Metro holidays.
- **Bring:** a Tennessee driver's license showing a Davidson County address. You unload it yourself.
- **Paying:** cash works at East and Ezell Pike but not at Omohundro. Check or card works everywhere, and cards add a 2.8% fee (minimum $1.95).
- **Recycled:** Metro says mattresses dropped off at a Convenience Center are recycled.

Heads up: the free "3 bags plus 1 bulk item" visit **doesn't cover mattresses**. You'll pay the mattress fee even if it's the only thing you bring.

## Can't haul it? Schedule a curbside pickup

Metro's bulky item page says to contact the **Davidson County Sheriff's Office** for curbside pickup of bulky items, and mattresses and box springs are on the Sheriff's list.

- **Call 615-880-3897** to schedule. You'll be asked to list the items and give the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request.
- **Cost:** the Sheriff's pages we read don't mention a fee, so ask when you call.
- **Front curb, by 6:30 a.m.** Crews have up to seven business days from the date you're given (weekends and holidays don't count), so ask when to set it out.
- **Apartment, condo or townhome?** The crews can't go onto complex property, so the Sheriff's Office asks that items go at the front curb by the property's main entrance.
- **It won't be recycled.** Metro says mattresses picked up at the curb aren't recycled. If you want yours recycled, take it to a Convenience Center.

Our [curbside pickup guide]({{ url('/nashville/bulk-trash/') }}) has the Sheriff's full list and rules.

## Recycle it

Metro's 2026 list of local recycling providers names **Springback** under mattresses: 615-207-2736. Call to ask how drop-off or pickup works.

## Buying a new one?

Many mattress stores and online sellers will take your old mattress away when they deliver the new one, usually for a fee. Ask before you check out, because it's often the easiest option of all.

## Donating

Charities are picky about used mattresses. Most won't take one with stains or tears, so call before you load it up.
