## Free: recycle it as scrap metal

Metro's examples include refrigerators, ovens and ranges, washers and dryers, dishwashers, barbecue grills and other large household appliances.

- **Cost:** one bulk item or up to three bags of scrap metal free. Extra loads are charged by volume ({{ fee_text('volume_small') }}).
- **Where:** any of the four centers: {{ centers() }}
- **Hours:** Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Closed on Metro holidays.
- **Bring:** a Tennessee driver's license with a Davidson County address, plus help lifting. You unload it yourself.

**Fridges, freezers and window AC units** hold refrigerant. The Metro pages we checked don't spell out a refrigerant rule, so call 615-862-5000 before you haul one over to avoid a wasted trip.

## Can't haul it? Schedule a curbside pickup

Metro's bulky item page says to contact the **Davidson County Sheriff's Office** for curbside pickup of bulky items. The Sheriff's list includes refrigerators, freezers, ranges, washers and dryers, water heaters, microwave ovens and air conditioners. Dishwashers aren't on its list, so ask when you call.

- **Call 615-880-3897** to schedule. You'll be asked to list the items and give the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request.
- **Cost:** the Sheriff's pages we read don't mention a fee, so ask when you call.
- **Front curb, by 6:30 a.m.** Crews have up to seven business days from the date you're given (weekends and holidays don't count), so ask when to set it out.
- **Fridge or freezer? Take the doors off before you set it out.** It could sit at the curb for days, and the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission warns that children can suffocate inside old refrigerators. If a door won't come off, the CPSC says to chain and padlock it shut.
- **Ask about refrigerant.** The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency says whoever finally disposes of a fridge, freezer or AC unit is responsible for recovering its refrigerant, so ask how it's handled when you call.
- **Apartment, condo or townhome?** The crews can't go onto complex property, so the Sheriff's Office asks that items go at the front curb by the property's main entrance.
- **It won't be recycled.** Metro says scrap metal picked up at the curb isn't recycled, but it is at a Convenience Center.

Our [curbside pickup guide]({{ url('/nashville/bulk-trash/') }}) has the Sheriff's full list and rules.

## Still works? Donate it

Habitat for Humanity's **ReStore** (414 Harding Place, 615-942-1291) sells donated appliances, and Habitat says most ReStores pick up large items. Call first, because each store's list of accepted donations is a little different.

## Buying a new one?

Ask the store whether delivery includes haul-away of the old one. It often costs less than hiring someone separately.
