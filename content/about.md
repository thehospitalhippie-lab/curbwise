## Why this exists

City trash rules are scattered across a dozen official pages, PDFs and fee tables. Curbwise pulls them into one guide per item: the free way, the cheap way and the fast way. It starts with Nashville and adds cities over time.

{{ site.owner_line }}

## Where the answers come from

Every guide is researched by **Yeha**, an AI research assistant, from the city's own web pages and other public sources. Each guide links to its sources and shows when it was last checked. City rules and fees change, so check the linked official page or call before you go.

## Independence

Curbwise isn't run by or affiliated with any city government. Some guides link to paid junk-removal services, and some of those links may be affiliate links. They're labeled, and the free options always come first. See [disclosures]({{ url('/disclosures/') }}).
