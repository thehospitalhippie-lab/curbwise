How to get rid of a refrigerator, washer or other appliance in Nashville

Metro recycles big appliances as scrap metal at its Convenience Centers, and one per visit is free.

Checked Nov 5, 2026 against official Nashville pages · Researched by Yeha, an AI assistant

Quick answer: Take it to any Convenience Center. Metro recycles appliances as scrap metal, and one bulk item (or up to three bags) is free. Working appliances can go to a ReStore.

Free: recycle it as scrap metal

Metro’s examples include refrigerators, ovens and ranges, washers and dryers, dishwashers, barbecue grills and other large household appliances.

  • Cost: one bulk item or up to three bags of scrap metal free. Extra loads are charged by volume ($10 at All four centers (Up to 2 visits a day; load can't exceed the truck bed)).
  • Where: any of the four centers: Anderson Lane Convenience Center — 939A Anderson Ln, Madison, TN 37115East Convenience Center — 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207Ezell Pike Convenience Center — 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211Omohundro Convenience Center — 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210
  • Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.
  • Bring: a Tennessee driver’s license with a Davidson County address, plus help lifting. You unload it yourself.

Fridges, freezers and window AC units hold refrigerant. Metro’s pages don’t spell out a refrigerant rule, so call 615-862-5000 before you haul one over to avoid a wasted trip.

Still works? Donate it

Habitat for Humanity’s ReStore (414 Harding Place, 615-942-1291) takes donated appliances to resell, and most ReStores pick up large items. Call first, because each store sets its own rules on age and condition.

Buying a new one?

Ask the store whether delivery includes haul-away of the old one. It often costs less than hiring someone separately.

Rather not lift a thing?

Paid junk-removal services pick up from inside your home or the curb, and many donate usable items. It costs more than dropping it off yourself, but it's the fastest option.

  • LoadUp — Book online with an upfront price; pickup from inside your home or outside.
  • College HUNKS Hauling Junk — Nashville junk removal; book online or get a free estimate.
