How to get rid of anything in Nashville, TN

Most big or tricky items go to one of Nashville's four Convenience Centers. Here's where they are, what each takes, what it costs, and what to bring.

The four Convenience Centers

  • Anderson Lane Convenience Center — 939A Anderson Ln, Madison, TN 37115
  • East Convenience Center — 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
  • Ezell Pike Convenience Center — 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
  • Omohundro Convenience Center — 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Closed on Metro holidays.

Before you load the car

  • Bring a Tennessee driver's license that shows a Davidson County address.
  • You unload your own load.
  • Household (residential) material only — no commercial waste, and no vehicles with commercial advertising or commercial tags.
  • Household trash must be bagged.
  • Payment: cash (not at Omohundro), check, or card. Cards carry a 2.8% fee, minimum $1.95. Prices are set by Metro and aren't negotiable.

Not accepted at any center

Ammunition · Asbestos (shingles, flooring, etc.) · Brick, concrete, masonry, rock, dirt · Brush and leaves · Car parts (other than tires) · Chain-link and rebar · Fireworks and flares · Gas tanks · Medical waste · Pallets, poles, posts, railroad ties · Plumbing, gutters, siding, roofing shingles · Radioactive material

Questions: call the Nashville Department of Waste Services at 615-862-5000.
