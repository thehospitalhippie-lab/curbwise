How to get rid of old tires in Nashville

Metro takes up to four tires a month at two Convenience Centers. Without rims they're free.

Checked Nov 5, 2026 against official Nashville pages · Researched by Yeha, an AI assistant

Quick answer: Up to 4 tires a month go to the East or Ezell Pike Convenience Center. Tires without rims are free. Tires still on rims cost $5 each.

The details

  • Without rims: Free at East, Ezell Pike (4 tires a month).
  • With rims: $5 per tire at East, Ezell Pike (4 tires a month).
  • Where: East Convenience Center — 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207Ezell Pike Convenience Center — 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
  • Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Closed on Metro holidays.
  • Bring: a Tennessee driver’s license with a Davidson County address. These are for household tires only, not from a business.
  • Not accepted: other car parts. Metro takes tires but no other automotive parts.

Getting new tires?

Tire shops usually take your old tires when they put new ones on, often for a small disposal fee. If you’re replacing them anyway, that’s the easiest route.

Rather not lift a thing?

Paid junk-removal services pick up from inside your home or the curb, and many donate usable items. It costs more than dropping it off yourself, but it's the fastest option.

  • LoadUp — Book online with an upfront price; pickup from inside your home or outside.
  • College HUNKS Hauling Junk — Nashville junk removal; book online or get a free estimate.
