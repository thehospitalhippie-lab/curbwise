Affiliate links

Curbwise is free to use. Some guides link to paid junk-removal and hauling services for people who’d rather not haul things themselves. When one of those is an affiliate link and you book through it, Curbwise may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are labeled and marked sponsored in the page code. Free and city options always come first.

Privacy

  • This site doesn’t use cookies, ads or tracking pixels.

  • It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Accuracy

Guides are built from official city pages and dated. Rules and fees change, so check the official source linked at the bottom of each guide, or call, before you go.
